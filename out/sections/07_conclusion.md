## Conclusion

Nexus provides a unified cognitive architecture that addresses the fragmentation between reasoning traces and long-term memory by treating memory as a dynamic graph and tools as composable functions. By enabling recursive tool-chaining grounded in persistent memory, Nexus moves beyond the limitations of transient prompt context [Yao et al., 2023] and single-step tool execution [Schick et al., 2023]. We hypothesize that this integration will enhance performance on complex multi-step tasks and improve social coherence. Future research will focus on optimizing the retrieval mechanism and scaling the recursive depth of tool-use to further minimize hallucination and enhance autonomous decision-making in open-world environments.
